Operational Methodology

Four steps. Three minutes. Fully offline.

  1. 01

    Capture the field

    Staff capture ground realities via lightweight mobile apps with time, location, and verified media stamped on-device.

  2. 02

    On-device inference

    Matching and telemetry processing occur locally on the phone. No sensitive personal data leaves the local hardware without encryption.

  3. 03

    Human confirmation

    Unmatched records flag for quick supervisor review. Human judgment remains final; honest field work gains an undeniable record.

  4. 04

    Reconcile & report

    Records sync automatically when connectivity resumes. Discrepancies are reported as pure arithmetic for district leadership.
