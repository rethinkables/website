The product

EduAttend.AI

Verified attendance and mid-day meal reconciliation for government schools.

Verified, not typed

A full class is recorded in about three minutes on an ordinary smartphone — presence captured by verified means, not entered by hand.

Works offline

No dependence on rural connectivity. Records are held on the device and sync automatically the moment a network appears.

Cannot be altered

Every record is written once. It cannot be edited or backdated — so the number a district sees is the number that was taken.

Meals reconciled nightly

Each night, meal claims are checked against verified attendance and any discrepancy is reported as arithmetic — never as accusation.

Consent by design

Student data is enrolled only with written parental consent under the DPDP Act, 2023. Facial matching happens on the device; no images leave the phone.

Built for the teacher

A support, not a monitor. No punitive mechanism — the teacher's judgment is always final, and honest work gains a verifiable record.

How it works

Four steps. Three minutes. Fully offline.

  1. 01

    Scan the class

    The teacher scans a code at the classroom. The roster, the period, and the assigned subject load — with time and place stamped.

  2. 02

    Sweep the room

    A single pass of the camera matches faces on the device itself. Nothing leaves the phone but roll numbers and present-or-absent.

  3. 03

    Confirm the rest

    Anyone unmatched begins as absent. The teacher confirms with a tap — presence is always earned, never assumed.

  4. 04

    Reconcile & report

    Records sync when online. Meal claims are reconciled, morning-to-afternoon variance is measured, and the district sees verified figures.
